The real problem

Most businesses don't have a lead problem. They have a leak.

Customers are already looking for what you sell. Somewhere between their first search and their first payment, most of them quietly slip away to someone else.

  • They can't find you

    You're not on page one of Google, not on the map, and not in the answers ChatGPT and other AI assistants give.

  • They find you, but don't trust you

    An outdated site, no reviews, no clear price or next step. They hit back and call the next name on the list.

  • They ask, and nobody answers

    The message lands at 21:00, or while you're with a client. By morning they've booked with whoever replied first.

  • They're interested, then forgotten

    No reminders, no follow-up, enquiries scattered across WhatsApp, email and sticky notes. Warm prospects go cold.

  • They buy once and disappear

    No review request, no rebooking, no referral ask. Your happiest customers never bring you their friends.
